India's midday surplus: ₹0 power, curtailed solar, and a ₹10 evening

Solar capacity grew 24-fold in a decade. At midday the grid now regularly has more power than it can use or move. Exchange prices collapse, Grid-India orders plants to back down, and a few hours later the same market hits its price cap.

Sources: CEA, MNRE, Grid-India, IEX, CERC, Ember, IEEFA/JMK. 470 sources registered. Derived figures are labelled.

Graph 1

Capacity grew fast, and generation kept pace

  • Solar went from 5.7 to 136 GW (2015→2025, Ember) and generation from 7 to 195 TWh.
  • Solar output per GW has held near 19% a year since 2022. The monthly figure in mid-2025 and mid-2026 is the lowest since 2018, when curtailment peaked.

Graph 2

More wind and solar, cheaper midday power

  • Since 2021 solar-hour prices have sat below other hours. The gap reached ₹4.24/kWh in 2026.
  • Very low prices also happened in 2015–17, but at night. Since 2024 they come at midday: 1,329 of 2025's 1,443 low blocks fell between 09:00 and 17:00.
  • In the 15-minute data, once wind and solar pass about 30% of demand, the median day-ahead price drops below ₹2/kWh.

Graph 3

Curtailment is outrunning storage and transmission

  • Official curtailment data starts only in January 2025. The worst month was October 2025 at 952 GWh.
  • Batteries total 14.5 GWh, about a tenth of the worst curtailment day.
  • About 19 GW in Rajasthan and Khavda is connected but lacks firm transmission access.

What the cross-checks show

Seven leads

Curtailment and near-zero prices are the same event

On days with more than 60 GWh curtailed, the median solar-hour day-ahead price was ₹0.81/kWh, and sell bids were 5.5 times buy bids. 88% of those days had a block at ≤ ₹1.

Spearman ρ −0.64 across 592 days.

Coal kept running while solar was switched off

On the heaviest curtailment days, midday thermal output was still about 110 GW, 67% of that day's peak. It then ramped about 49 GW into the evening. Grid frequency reached 50.2 Hz or more on 60% of those days.

Ask: unit-level on-bar data and must-run decisions.

The reason column hides transmission

Official reports name a transmission constraint for only 3% of curtailed energy. 73% is emergency "TRAS down" for grid security. Yet the most-curtailed sites are the Rajasthan and Khavda pooling stations that Grid-India's own access reports show to be short of transmission.

The label decides who pays. Pending: CERC 846/MP/2025, 26/MP/2026.

Some curtailment is never counted

At the tightest midday block, up to 6.7 GW in Rajasthan and 8.9 GW at Khavda is connected but not allowed to inject. These are planned limits, not real-time orders, and they don't appear in the curtailment tables.

Official totals are probably a floor.

₹0 needs a holiday, for now

The real-time market's ₹0 days fell on May Day (demand 33 GW below the week before), a Sunday (−25 GW, 102 GWh curtailed) and an ordinary Tuesday with 89.6 GW of wind and solar.

The official reports for two of these days can't be obtained (see A1).

Floor-to-cap days are new

Days with a midday price at ≤ ₹1 and an evening at the ₹10 cap: one in 2022, none in 2023, then 14 in 2024, 45 in 2025 and 44 in 2026 so far. Battery tariffs are about ₹2.1/kWh. Of the 35 GWh of subsidised (VGF) battery storage awarded, 0.5 GWh is commissioned.

The surplus outlived the demand slump

2025 demand was weak, down 6% year on year in October, the record curtailment month. 2026 demand rebounded 11–21%, but curtailment stayed high. Monsoon wind became 31% of curtailment in July 2026.

What isn't available

Curtailment
No official series before January 2025. The energy lost under T-GNA limits isn't reported anywhere.

Forecasts
Grid-India and the state dispatch centres publish no forecast-error statistics (MAPE or NRMSE), even though the 2023 grid code requires them.

Demand
No official 15-minute or hourly all-India series before 4 Nov 2024.

Prices
No day-ahead data before April 2012, and no regional prices for the real-time market.

Capacity
MNRE's monthly Physical Progress archive wasn't collected; CEA reports were used instead. State-level wind before 2024 covers only the top five states.

Storage
No official battery history before 2025, and no list of projects.
